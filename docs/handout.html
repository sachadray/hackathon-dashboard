3. The data

Land use in Viet Nam's 63 provinces (before the 2025 merger), from the National Statistics Office. Full data dictionary.

FileWhat it holds
★ land_provinces.csvArea in thousand hectares by province and category, 2018 and 2020 to 2023. Categories: agri farmland, forest, special specially used land (industry, roads, public works), homestead residential land, total.
★ vn_provinces.geojsonProvince shapes for a map, joined to the CSV on iso.
land_national.csvThe whole country by category, 2008 to 2023.
land_finance.csvLand revenue (575.5 trillion dong in 2025), GDP, the rice-land floor and the 2030 plan targets, each with its source.

Watch out: Da Nang's figures have a break in 2022 (leave it out of changes). Binh Duong and Binh Phuoc have a blank 2021 farmland value.

Public financial management scores for 194 economies, built from 68 international indicators (PEFA, Open Budget Survey and others). Full data dictionary.

FileWhat it holds
★ scores.csvOne row per economy: D1 budget preparation, D2 budget execution, D3 transparency, accountability and oversight, D4 budget reliability and perceived effectiveness, overall, and 19 sub-dimension scores.
★ economies.csvEconomy names, income group, region, years of the latest PEFA assessment and Open Budget Survey.
indicators.csvThe 85 indicators: name, unit, dimension, source and link.
indicator_values.csvRaw value and z-score for each indicator and economy.
gdp_per_capita.csvGDP per capita 2025, US dollars (IMF).

Every score is a z-score: 0 is the average of all economies with data, +1 is one standard deviation better. A score of −0.4 is below average, not a failing grade.

If something goes wrong

  • The dashboard does not appear in the chat. Ask: "Give me the complete HTML file." Save the text as dashboard.html and open it in your browser.
  • The map is empty. Ask it to check the join between iso in the CSV and iso in the GeoJSON.
  • The numbers differ from the known value. Ask: "Show me the calculation step by step for Quang Ninh" (or Viet Nam). The mistake is usually there.
  • The file is too large to attach. You only need the two starred files.
